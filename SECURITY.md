# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Do not open a public issue.**

Write to **hello@6s.lu**, with `SECURITY` in the subject line.

An issue in this repository is public and permanent the moment it is created.
A vulnerability posted there is disclosed to everyone, including to people who
will use it, before there is a fix. Email keeps it between us until there is.

## What helps

- Which product, and which version and platform.
- What an attacker can actually do with it — that is what decides how fast it
  gets fixed.
- The steps to reproduce it. A proof of concept is welcome; please do not test
  against anyone else's data or account.

## What to expect

6S is one maker, so there is no 24-hour acknowledgement to promise. What we
will do:

- reply as soon as we have read and understood it, usually within a few days;
- tell you plainly whether we agree it is a vulnerability, and if not, why;
- tell you when it is fixed and in which version;
- credit you by name when it is, if you would like that, and not if you would
  not.

We will not threaten you with legal action for reporting something in good
faith, and we will not ask you to stay quiet about it indefinitely.

## Scope

Every 6S product, and the sites 6s.lu, 4s.lu, 5s.lu, blacktulip.lu and
pay.6s.lu.

## A note on the products

**BEWEEG** handles patient adherence data and **6S-PAY** handles payment
sessions. Anything touching either is treated as serious by default, whatever
the severity looks like at first glance.
