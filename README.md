## What a useful report contains

Three things, and the third is the one people leave out:

1. **What you did** — the steps, in order, from opening the app.
2. **What you expected to happen.**
3. **What happened instead** — the exact message if there was one, a screenshot
   if there wasn't.

Also useful: the app version, your device and OS version. The issue form asks
for all of it, so you do not have to remember.

## ⚠️ What not to put in a public issue

Everything here is **public and permanent**, readable by anyone, indexed by
search engines. Please do not post:

- passwords, API keys, recovery codes or payment details
- your address, phone number, or anyone else's
- **anything about your health, your prescription or your treatment** — this
  matters most for BEWEEG, which is used alongside prescribed activity. A
  support answer is never worth putting your medical information on the public
  internet.
- **security vulnerabilities** — see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); they go to
  hello@6s.lu, never here.

If what you need to say does not belong in public, write to **hello@6s.lu**
instead. Nothing is lost by doing that.

## What happens next

1. Your issue is labelled `triage` and with its product.
2. We reproduce it, or ask for whatever is missing. Then it is `confirmed`.
3. The actual work happens in the product's own private repository. Code and
   logs stay private; this issue stays the public thread.
4. When the fix ships, we comment here with the version it went out in, label
   it `shipped`, and close it.

**We close things out loud.** A closed issue with no comment reads as being
ignored, and it should not.

## What to expect

6S is one maker. Most things get a first reply within a few days. Nothing here
is a support contract, and we would rather say that than imply otherwise.

## Your issues are yours

What you write here stays yours. By posting you allow us to use it to
understand and fix the problem, and to quote it when we explain what changed.
